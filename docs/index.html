ExpenseTracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

1. Overview

ExpenseTracker ("the App") is a personal finance application developed by Bruno Brandão. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

ExpenseTracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data you enter into the App — including transactions, categories, notes, and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using a local Room database and Android DataStore.

No data is sent to external servers, third-party services, or the developer.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device solely to provide its features:

  • Transaction records (amount, type, category, date, optional notes)
  • User preferences (selected currency, theme, date format)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the CSV Export feature, which saves a file to your device's Downloads folder under your full control.

4. CSV Export

The App offers an optional CSV Export feature that writes your transaction data to a file in your device's Downloads folder. This file is created locally and is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere by the App. You are solely responsible for how you share or store that file.

5. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions necessary for its core functionality. No network, camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions are requested or used.

6. Third-Party Services

ExpenseTracker does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for use by all age groups.

8. Data Security

Because all data remains on your device, its security depends on the security of your device itself (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device software up to date and using a strong lock screen.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete individual transactions at any time within the App. Uninstalling the App will permanently remove all locally stored data from your device.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be published at this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
